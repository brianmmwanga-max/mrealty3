The Firm

Thirteen years,
one standard of evidence.

M Realty was founded in 2013 on the belief that residential advice should look more like an investment memo than a brochure. We still publish that way.

Origin

Built from development sales, not property management.

Our first mandate was eleven apartments in a Kilimani tower the market had written off — sold in fourteen weeks by publishing the building's actual service records next to the competition's adjectives. That method became the firm.

Today M Realty runs three desks: Development Sales (Gemini and The Quarter are under exclusive mandate), Prime Resale, and an Investment Advisory practice preparing acquisition cases for local, diaspora, and regional buyers.

House Standards

Four rules we do not break.

01

Evidence over adjectives

Every listing carries measurable facts: ceiling heights, parking ratios, service charges, title status.

02

One named adviser

From first valuation to completion you deal with a person, not a pool. Their record is on this page.

03

Conservative pricing

We decline overpriced mandates. A listing held too long costs the seller more than our fee.

04

Quiet where possible

A third of our transactions never appear on the public register. Off-market access is by request.
