How We Work

A short book,
held to a long standard.

M Realty was founded on a simple dissatisfaction: listings described in adjectives instead of specifications. We publish ceiling heights, parking ratios, acoustic test results, and service histories — because that is what determines whether an asset holds its value in fifteen years.

We take fewer mandates than our competitors and hold them longer. Sellers work with one named adviser from first valuation to completion; buyers receive written comparables before they are shown a door.
