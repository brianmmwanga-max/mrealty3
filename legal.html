Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: October 2026.

M Realty Ltd ("we", "the firm") collects only what is needed to respond to your enquiry: your name, email, phone (optional), the property or interest you mention, and any message you choose to include. Enquiries submitted through this website are delivered by our form backend to and, where enabled, mirrored as a WhatsApp notification to the firm's business line.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. Data is retained for 24 months after last contact, then deleted. Newsletter registrations ("Off-Market Access") can be revoked at any time by replying UNSTOP to any message or emailing the above address.

This site uses no advertising trackers and no third-party analytics cookies. Saved Properties are stored in your own browser's local storage and never leave your device unless you submit an enquiry.
