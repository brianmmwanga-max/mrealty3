Buying off-plan, without the folklore.

Off-plan is not speculation when the structure is verified. This is how M Realty underwrites every phased purchase we represent — and what we ask of any developer, including the ones we are not retained by.

The five checks we run before recommending an off-plan unit

  • Title & encumbrance search — registry-verified, not photocopies.
  • Developer track record — at least one completed, occupied scheme of comparable scale.
  • Independent quantity-surveyor certification of draw-down milestones.
  • Escrow with a regulated account — funds released against certified progress only.
  • Exit liquidity test — can this unit be resold at handover? We model it before you buy.

How phased payment actually works

A disciplined Kenyan off-plan schedule commits roughly 30% at allocation, 30% at structural completion, and the balance against finishes and handover. Anything that asks for more than 30% before foundation sign-off should be questioned — regardless of who is asking.

Mortgage buyers typically draw down at the structure stage. We coordinate the valuation calendar with your bank so interest does not accrue on idle equity.
